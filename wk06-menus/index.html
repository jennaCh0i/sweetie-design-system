Advanced Interface · WK06 · Menus

Menu system

One menu item structure, two tones. Every row is built from the same slots: an optional left icon, a single line of text, and one right-side accessory (a shortcut, a badge, an icon or a submenu chevron). Rows group into sections with a header and a separator, and any row can open a submenu. Serene is the account menu on the profile icon; Elegant is the menu on each past order in Order history.

01 · Serene

Account menu

Tone: calm, soft, unhurried. The menu is a sage card with generous corners; rows are rounded pills that warm to white on hover and settle into green when pressed. It opens with a slow fade and rise, rows arriving one after another. Function: it drops from the profile icon and gathers everything about Matt’s account — order history, rewards, the bag, settings, dietary preferences and pickup store — with ⌘ shortcuts for an iPad keyboard.

    02 · Elegant

    Order menu

    Tone: precise, quiet, editorial. A cream sheet with a hairline edge and small corners; light type in Sweetie green, rows divided by hairlines and sections by a thin band. It appears quickly with no stagger, and its submenu slides out from the side. Function: tap ··· on any past order, right-click it, or press and hold — then reorder, edit, view the receipt, rate, share, favorite or hide it.
