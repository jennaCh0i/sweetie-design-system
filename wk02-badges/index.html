Serene badges & tooltips

Five sets in one tone, each a badge and a tooltip anchored to a real Sweetie element, plus an extra tooltip on the move-canvas toggle. Badges go from no badge → dot or no badge → content, and back; the carrot starts with its badge. Tooltips go from anchor default → hovered, revealing a plain tooltip or a rich tooltip with an action. Everything fades in from a soft blur, settles slowly and never glows.

tone serenesettle 1.2sdrift 0.7sbreath 4.8sease cubic-bezier(.45,0,.2,1)
Click a badge anchor (or use the buttons) to show and clear its badge. Hover or focus a tooltip anchor to reveal its tooltip; rich tooltips stay open while you’re on them, and Escape closes them. With reduced motion turned on, every state still appears, just without the slow fades.
