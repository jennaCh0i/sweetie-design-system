List item system

One list item structure, two tones. The same slots build every row in Sweetie: a leading icon, avatar, bowl thumbnail or nothing; one or two lines of text; trailing text, an icon or both. Rows either navigate one level deeper or expand to reveal sub-items, and every interactive row has Enabled, Hover and Pressed states.
