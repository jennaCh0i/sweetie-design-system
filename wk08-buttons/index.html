Advanced Interface · WK08 · Buttons

Button system

One button structure, three levels of emphasis, two tones. Every button is built from the same parts: an optional icon and an optional label inside a container. The level says how important the action is: Primary is filled with Sweetie green for the one action that finishes the task, Secondary has a soft low-contrast fill for an alternative, and Tertiary is transparent with an outline for optional actions. A button group joins several into a single choice. Serene runs the Bag and checkout; Elegant runs the order details in Order history.

01 · Serene

Bag & checkout buttons

Tone: calm, soft, reassuring. Paying is the moment people hesitate, so every button is a full pill, the colours stay in Sweetie’s sage family, and changes take their time: fills warm on hover, sink into green when pressed, and the group’s cream indicator glides to the new choice. The tablet is the ordering kiosk in the store. Function: Pay · total sends you to the card reader (primary), Add another bowl keeps you ordering and Add (rewards) looks you up by phone (secondary), the bin and − step a bowl down (tertiary). The quantity buttons are icon only, and For here · To go is a single-choice group (To go turns on utensils).

02 · Elegant

Order details buttons

Tone: precise, quiet, editorial. Pills like every Sweetie button, but drawn with hairline outlines, cream fills, light type and quick, exact changes; colour is held back until it means something, like the filled heart or a chosen rating. Function: Reorder adds the past bowl to your bag (primary), Send rating confirms your feedback and is disabled until you pick one (secondary), View receipt opens the receipt in place (tertiary). Favorite and Share are icon only, and How was it? is a single-choice group.
