Advanced Interface · WK07 · Toolbars

Toolbar system

One toolbar structure, two tones. A toolbar is a container that holds buttons, and every button is built the same way: an icon, a label, or both. Dividers group related actions, groups sit on the left or right of a bar, and the whole toolbar can align left, centre or right. Serene floats over the Chat with ingredients canvas; Elegant lives in Order history, in the header and as a selection bar.

01 · Serene

Canvas toolbar

Tone: calm, soft, unhurried. A sage pill that floats over the ingredients, with round buttons that warm to white on hover, sink into green when pressed, and turn solid green when a tool is on. Function: it’s how you get around the Chat with ingredients canvas. Move switches on the hand tool so you can drag the canvas, − and + zoom out and in, the percentage resets the zoom to 100%, and Recentre brings everything home. It sits centred at the bottom and slides to the middle of the free space when a chat opens.

    02 · Elegant

    Order history toolbars

    Tone: precise, quiet, editorial. A cream strip with a hairline edge; buttons are joined by hairline dividers rather than floating apart, the type is light, and changes happen quickly. Function: the header toolbar, aligned right beside the title, searches your orders, filters them (All orders, Favorites, a month) and switches into selection mode. In selection mode a second toolbar slides up from the bottom: the count and Select all on the left, Reorder, Share and Remove on the right.
