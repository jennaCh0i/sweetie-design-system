Advanced Interface · WK04 · Sliders

Sliders

In the bowl builder, every ingredient bar is a slider. Slide across a bar and it tells you how much you're getting: None, Less, Good amount, More, A lot. Slide down to None and the ingredient leaves your bowl. Three tones, each with one stepped slider and one continuous slider, all about the same thing: how much goes in your bowl.

01 Serene · Ingredient bars 02 Playful · Heat & tomatoes 03 Elegant · Avocado & olive oil → In the prototype
FunctionChoose an amount. Stepped sliders snap to named portions; continuous sliders pick any value in a range. Drag, tap the track, or use ← → Home End on the keyboard.
ValueEvery slider says its value: a bubble while you drag and a resting label (portion word, grams, ml or %).
ToneSerene (default Sweetie), Playful (toppings energy, elastic hexagon), Elegant (a single 1 px line).

01

Serene · Ingredient bars

The ingredient bar from the bowl builder is now the slider. Put your finger anywhere on the bar and slide: the colored fill follows you, small dots show where the portions are, and a deep green bubble names the portion you're on. Let go and the fill slowly settles on the nearest portion. The continuous version (dressing) uses the same bar with the Sweetie gradient and reads out a percentage.

Portion

5 stops: None · Less · Good amount · More · A lot. At None the bar empties back to cream. In the bowl builder, sliding to None and letting go removes the ingredient from the list. The fill always leaves room for the name (name + 56 px, at least 34%); the stops are spaced evenly up to 100%.

02

Playful · Heat & tomatoes

Toppings energy, carried over from the WK03 Playful toggle. The thumb is a Sweetie hexagon: it squashes when you grab it, springs past each stop and settles, and turns 30° every step. In the continuous tomato slider, cherry tomatoes pop onto the bar as you slide further, so you can see the amount as well as read it in grams.

03

Elegant · Avocado & olive oil

A tasting-menu feel in a single hairline, like the WK03 Elegant set. Thin ticks mark the stops, and the deep green line draws in behind a small cream ring. When you hold the thumb, a faint halo opens around it. The value is always written above in quiet type, and the label under the active stop turns to ink.
